About me

From the boardroom to the model.

I started in business administration because I wanted to understand how organisations work. I moved into analytics because I wanted to answer their questions with evidence.

My BBA gave me a grounding in finance, management and economics. My Master's in Business Analytics added the technical side: Python, machine learning, deep learning and statistical modelling. Today I use both to turn raw data into clear recommendations.

I've built a CNN that classifies brain tumours from MRI scans, analysed 14 years of global vehicle sales, and published econometric work on economic freedom and sustainable tourism. My Master's thesis predicts blood sugar spikes for people managing diabetes. I also teach undergraduates at FSM, which keeps my communication sharp.
